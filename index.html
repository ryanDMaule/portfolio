ABOUT

ME

I'm a software developer based in Wales, combining engineering and product design to turn ideas into thoughtful, intuitive digital experiences.

With professional experience in software engineering, I specialise in Android development, but my work spans Flutter, web applications, games and automation. I enjoy solving technical challenges and building software that's reliable, maintainable and genuinely useful.

PRODUCT DESIGNER

I've designed, developed and released my own applications, shaping everything from the initial concept to the final user experience. Alongside my Google UX Design certification, this hands-on work has developed my approach to interaction design, usability and creating products that feel intuitive to use.
